Application process

Three steps to
make your request.

Your application is not submitted until you send the prepared email and receive guidance from the accommodation office.

STEP 01

Complete your details

Provide your student number, programme, year of study and preferred room type.

STEP 02

Review and send

The form prepares an email on your device. Review it and press Send in your email app.

STEP 03

Hear from the office

The accommodation office can confirm availability, fees, deadlines and next steps.
