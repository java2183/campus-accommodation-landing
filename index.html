01 / The challenge

Accommodation applications are still too hard to manage.

Today, students often need to visit the accommodation office and rely on a manual process. Paperwork and disconnected updates make it harder for everyone to stay informed.

01

In-person applications

Students have to travel to the office just to begin or follow up on an application.

02

Paperwork is easy to lose

Manual records make it difficult to organise, retrieve, and review application details.

03

Unclear application status

Without a clear update path, students can be left unsure about what happens next.

04

Room and payment follow-up

Separate processes make it harder for staff to coordinate room decisions and payment records.

↗ Students lose time; accommodation teams lose a clear view of the process.

02 / Our proposed system

One connected place to manage the whole journey.

CampusNest is a proposed digital accommodation management system. It gives students a simpler way to apply and check progress, while helping staff keep applications, room allocation, and payment information organised.

A practical set of tools to support students and the teams who help them find a place to live.

＄
