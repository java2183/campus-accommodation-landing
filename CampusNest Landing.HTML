The Problem

What problem? Poor management of the accommodation application process. When students want to apply for accommodation, the process is not well organised.

Where? At the accommodation office, where students go to apply for a place to stay.

Who is affected? The students are the ones mostly affected, since they are the ones who have to apply for accommodation.

What currently happens? Students have to go in person to apply. This is done manually, with no proper system to manage applications, room allocation, or payments.

Why is it a problem? It takes up a lot of time, is inconvenient, and makes it harder for staff to keep track properly.
